⚠ One missable trophy — read this first

Positive Mental Attitude (bronze) asks you to meditate at every spot across Leonida — and one of the 18 spots is a balcony in Chapter 1. Meditate there before Chapter 1 ends, or it is gone for this playthrough. Everything else in this guide can be finished in free roam after the story.

Roadmap to platinum

  1. Phase 1 — Story. Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. Grab the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude on the way, and finish the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) as they appear.
  2. Phase 2 — Open-world feats. Stunts, races, drone races, shooting range golds, fishing with Cal, arcade, bowling, combat feats and robberies. Most of these stack naturally while you roam.
  3. Phase 3 — Collectibles. Card sets, WAiNK It locations, vehicles, all 52 areas of Leonida, garages and Scooter Bros registrations. Slow and steady — this is the longest grind.
  4. Phase 4 — Cleanup. Chase 100% completion for the gold A Long Road, then watch the platinum Vice Got a Price! pop. Five trophies are still unconfirmed — check their cards before grinding them.
