Roadmap to the platinum ↓

Roadmap to the platinum

Three steps, in order. The story first, then the 100% checklist, then cleanup. Nothing on the checklist is missable — the only thing you can lose is one meditation in Chapter 1.

Step 1 · 22 trophies

Play the story

Chapters 1–5. Every story trophy pops on its own — 12 chapter and mission trophies. While you play, do the one thing that can be missed: the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude. Start the side-character questlines as they open (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) and the partner trophies once both characters are playable, after Chapter 2's “Coffee Break”.

Step 2 · 24 trophies

Chase 100% completion

After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist: 100 optional missions, 200 vehicles scanned, 100 animals studied, 70 of 80 challenges, all robberies, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's target decks. This is where A Long Road lives — and most of the silver trophies.

Step 3 · 9 trophies

Cleanup

Finish what's left: the 9 activity types (Master of None), the 500 Criminal Profile actions, and the five trophies whose method is still unconfirmed at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) — check this guide's sources for updates. Then the platinum pops.

One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. The Chapter 1 one is on Jason's balcony: after the mission “Down to Earth”, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose “Accept”. It is gone forever after Chapter 1. (It cannot block A Long Road — 100% needs only 15 of 18 spots.)
